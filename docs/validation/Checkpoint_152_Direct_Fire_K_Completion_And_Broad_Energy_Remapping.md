# Checkpoint 152 — Direct-Fire K Completion and Broad Energy Remapping

Status: **candidate pending native Windows acceptance**.

CP152 is an execution/research checkpoint on the native-validated CP151 ×2 offensive/defensive point scale. It does not modify production C#, the source Technology Numerical Matrix, DEF/RES, Reactor progression, missiles, PDS, or defensive centers.

## Purpose

CP149–CP151 established that Kinetic is much healthier but still has unresolved delivery/technology-progression interactions, while Energy—especially TL6–TL9—requires broader remapping. CP152 therefore uses asymmetric research scope:

- **K lane:** complete the remaining sensitive five-dimensional space with a full 3^5 factorial at every TL: DAM, ACC, Standard Range, Maximum Range, APEN. K SPEN remains zero; TP/ammo/Space are fixed because prior work showed them non-binding for combat tuning.
- **Energy lane:** broad OA(243), strength-2 response surface at every TL across Low/Standard/Overload DAM, ACC, Standard/Maximum Range, Low/Standard/Overload TP, SPEN, and Strain Limit. Energy APEN remains zero. Energy Space is audited separately as legality/headroom because fixed Stage-A templates cannot convert free Space into invented AUX.
- **Joint lane:** after independent K/E merges, select three competitive-specialist candidates per family per TL and cross them 3×3 against all K-vs-E Stage-A contexts. This prevents K selection from being biased by a known-deficient inherited E ladder and vice versa.

## Technology progression rule

Intrinsic technological characteristics may remain unchanged across quiet TLs, but they may not regress with higher TL absent an explicit, documented tradeoff. Final ladder selection therefore treats unexplained ACC/range regression as invalid even when locally attractive mathematically.

## Fixed environment

Hull, Shield Capacity, Armor Capacity, Shield Regen=2, Armor Repair=2, GP Missile, integer-centered Swarmer, PDS, AUX, ECM/ECCM/Sensor, Reactor ladder, DEF and RES remain at the CP151 research center. This isolates direct-fire response.
