# Checkpoint 162 — Main and Auxiliary Reactor Joint Calibration and Stacking Safety Diagnostic

## Purpose

CP161 established the broad Reactor/TP response surface. CP162 narrows the Main Reactor to the local PF4 -1/0/+1 neighborhood at the accepted 6-Space footprint and performs the first full quantitative calibration of the Auxiliary Reactor.

The central design question is how much Tactical Power every ship should receive by default versus how much additional sustained power a player may purchase with scarce Installation Space. A second requirement is explicit stacking safety: multiple Auxiliary Reactors remain legal during the study, and the research engine must show whether Space economics alone prevent a power-stack exploit.

## Frozen authority

- CP160-PF4 remains the research execution baseline.
- CP161 native results are accepted diagnostic evidence.
- Production numerical authority is unchanged.
- The Concept is unchanged.
- Main/PDS/core-defense values and CP158/159/160 AUX magnitudes are unchanged.
- Main Reactor Space is fixed at 6 for this pass.

## Study surface

- Main Reactor Operational TP: PF4 -1, PF4, PF4 +1 at every TL.
- Auxiliary Reactor: 1/2/3/4 Space x +1/+2/+3/+4 Operational TP.
- Auxiliary Reactor availability: TL1+.
- Auxiliary Reactor count: unrestricted by rule during the study.
- Static layer: every legal count from zero through the maximum allowed by remaining Installation Space.
- Stochastic/combat tiers: one, two, three, and maximum legal copies, de-duplicated when tiers coincide.
- Six CP161 tactical-demand doctrines retained.
- Full-map combat: matched carrier with N Auxiliary Reactors versus the identical carrier with zero Auxiliary Reactors, mirrored, for Kinetic/Energy/Missile and all three Main-Reactor offsets.

## Interpretation guardrails

A high-output Auxiliary Reactor is not acceptable merely because it improves a power-hungry ship. The evidence must expose TP per Space, the number and Space required to match a full Main Reactor's output, marginal benefit from additional copies, shortfall collapse, and combat response. An explicit installation-count cap may be recommended only if reasonable Space/TP economics cannot bound stacking on their own.

The player-base-cruiser envelope is reported separately from the broad legal envelope. A TL1 shieldless specialist may be a legal stress build; it is not treated as the standard player cruiser. Conversely, unusual legal builds remain in the study because they are precisely where a stacking exploit may appear.

## Planned scale

- 22,482 pre-existing legal powered architectures retained as the construction reference.
- 16 Auxiliary Reactor Space/TP candidates.
- 2,496 exact aggregate static legal-stack support rows.
- 8,280 stochastic variants x 2,000 turns = **16,560,000 turn-demand samples**.
- 2,760 mirrored combat contexts x three Main-Reactor offsets = 8,280 cells.
- 500 trials/cell = **4,140,000 substantive combats**.

No selection, tuning, PF5 promotion, or production promotion occurs in CP162.
