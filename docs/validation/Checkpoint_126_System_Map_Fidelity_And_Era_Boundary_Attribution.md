# Checkpoint 126 — System Map Fidelity and Era-Boundary Attribution

## Validation intent

CP126 is the required fidelity/attribution checkpoint between accepted CP125 pure-TL whole-ladder evidence and any future mixed-TL ecology study.

It changes no technology value. The only production mechanics correction is the removal of absolute-coordinate handedness from equal-choice finite ship movement and Missile pursuit. The research consumer then restores the accepted full finite-map encounter model and measures the CP125 sensitivity signals under that higher-fidelity geometry.

## RepositoryOnly acceptance

RepositoryOnly must pass, in order:

1. Python 3.13 and pinned .NET SDK 8.0.423 resolution;
2. pre-package hygiene;
3. CP126 preflight and **160/160 Python tests**;
4. warning-as-error native build;
5. **907/907 xUnit tests**, including shared System Map geometry parity/mirror tests;
6. **70/70 ScenarioRunner self-tests**;
7. **25/25 accepted C#/Python research parity fixtures**;
8. exact 9,427-build / 25,678-task / 139,000-variant plan reconstruction;
9. **2,250 physical side/mover symmetry comparisons / 4,500 combat executions with zero mismatches**;
10. **139,000 one-trial full-map smoke engagements with zero trial errors**;
11. repository/evidence contract and manifest verification.

RepositoryOnly runs no substantive balance workload.

## Normal acceptance

The normal invocation repeats all RepositoryOnly gates, then executes:

- **139,000 variants**;
- **250 trials per variant**;
- **34,750,000 substantive engagements**;
- zero trial errors required.

Balance outcomes cannot fail the checkpoint. The study exists to measure them.

## Interpretation boundary

The main review questions are:

- how much of CP125 adjacent-TL progression survives full geometry;
- why TL5->6 exceeded TL4->5 in CP125, separating same-composition gains from newly legal TL6 designs;
- whether TL7->8 behaves like the intended high-tech era entry once family effects are separated;
- whether TL2/TL7 movement-order cliffs persist off-axis;
- whether mature Swarmer retains an anti-PDS niche at TL8/TL9 without requiring an automatic upgrade;
- whether late Energy strength is primarily Shield-facing;
- whether TL8/TL9 Missile stalemates survive actual finite-map pursuit/rerouting;
- whether physical side/mover symmetry remains exact after the geometry/RNG corrections.

No result automatically changes the Tech Table. Mixed-TL ships remain deferred until CP126 is reviewed.
