# Checkpoint 156 — Research Continuity, Promotion Audit, and Future-Pass Guardrail Consolidation

Status: **candidate pending native Windows acceptance**.

CP156 is intentionally a **zero-combat continuity checkpoint**. It follows native-accepted CP155 and exists because Star Cluster now has a large body of expensive multivariate research that must remain usable even when values are not yet promoted into production numerical authority.

The governing rule is:

> **Not promoted does not mean not preserved.**

CP156 adds no production C#, changes no Concept rule, changes no active Technology Numerical Matrix value, and performs no tuning. Instead it freezes the evidence chain, records what has been established, records what remains provisional, audits whether a numerical promotion was ever actually authorized, and turns the project’s balance philosophy into machine-checkable future-pass guardrails.

## Promotion audit

The audit distinguishes three separate concepts:

1. **Active numerical/mechanical authority** — what the game/repository currently declares as authoritative.
2. **Native-validated research evidence** — response surfaces, sensitivity findings, interactions, and architecture conclusions established by accepted research checkpoints.
3. **Provisional carry-forward candidates** — viable ladders/values that remain candidates because later AUX or Reactor/TP economics can still affect the final choice.

CP149 through CP155 all explicitly set `automaticPromotion=false` and `tuningAllowed=false`. Therefore their K/E/M/Swarmer and PDS ladders were **not missed numerical promotions**. Their correct status is native-validated research evidence plus provisional candidate envelopes until a later explicit promotion checkpoint reconciles remaining dependencies.

The active numerical matrix remains separately hash-locked. Its existing historical changes — including the canonical point domain, CP133-era candidate profiles, CP135 Shield recharge, and CP137 Armor reserve — are audited as already present rather than overwritten by recent research overlays.

## Durable methodological guardrails

CP156 makes the following future-pass rules explicit and testable:

- Balance means differentiated, strategically viable choices, **not equal win rates**.
- Never optimize K-PDS, E-PDS, and AMM toward 50% at every TL; never minimize inter-family win-rate variance as a global balance target.
- Preserve the intentional lopsided Missile threat against ships that decline PDS.
- GP Missile and Swarmer must remain separately observable; their PDS responses must not be averaged away.
- Technology-valid whole-TL progression is mandatory. Quiet TLs and leapfrogging are allowed; arbitrary sawtooth regressions are not.
- Closed response surfaces are not silently reopened. A later pass must name the changed dependency and justify why prior evidence is no longer applicable.
- Energy-main Low/Standard/Overload and Energy-PDS overcharge remain family-identity mechanics; K/M do not receive generic Energy-style overload merely for numerical convenience.
- K/AMM ammunition is primarily endurance/logistics once practical thresholds are met, not a fine per-TL tuning axis.
- Final Reactor/TP supply is tuned only after AUX demand is established.
- Player-facing rules remain KISS; research analysis may remain multivariate and Pareto-oriented.
