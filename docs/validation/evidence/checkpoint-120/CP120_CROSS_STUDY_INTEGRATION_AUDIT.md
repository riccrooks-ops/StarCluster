## CP120 integration boundaries

CP120 adds only a new diagnostic study definition, one sensitivity-analysis module, CP120 tests, a shared-CLI parser/dispatch extension, and checkpoint/evidence/documentation infrastructure. The new study freezes the simplified weapon mechanics established by CP117-CP119:

- GP Missile candidates vary yield only; diagnostic GP SPEN/APEN stay fixed.
- Swarmer remains TL2+, exactly two packets, one Missile Flight, one terminal attack, and bounded coverage/PDS-saturation characteristics.
- Kinetic candidates remain automatic single-axis controls; no normal ammunition-selection menu or packet/tandem mechanics return.
- Energy is a native unchanged reference family.
- TL1-TL6 numerically dominate the study population; TL7 is advanced validation and TL8-TL9 are endpoint/stress evidence.

## Acceptance implication

A CP120 native acceptance failure should be diagnosed as a CP120 study/integration/checkpoint issue unless a parity fixture or retained-study regression smoke also fails. Earlier accepted Monte Carlo evidence remains authoritative for the mechanics it exercised. CP120 contains no automatic numerical promotion.
