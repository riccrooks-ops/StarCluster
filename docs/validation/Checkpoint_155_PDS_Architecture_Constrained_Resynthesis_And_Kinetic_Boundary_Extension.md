# Checkpoint 155 — PDS Architecture-Constrained Resynthesis and Kinetic Boundary Extension

Status: **candidate pending native Windows acceptance**.

CP155 follows native-accepted CP154 CR1. CP154 remains valid broad PDS response-surface evidence: all 32,729,400 substantive combats completed with zero substantive execution errors, and it established that RC is a major architectural breakpoint, Energy overcharged RC2 is a genuine intermediate state controlled by Strain, and AMM RC3/range-1 is a mechanically meaningful unique interception window. CP155 does **not** repeat that broad oversweep.

The reason for CP155 is methodological. CP154's final shortlist scalarization placed substantial weight on aggregate distance from a 50% defender decisive share. Because that aggregate included missile-armed defenders receiving PDS, it unintentionally favored weak PDS and pruned K RC2 and Energy overcharged-RC2 architectures before deep confirmation. That selection objective contradicted the established Star Cluster balance philosophy and the earlier v17-v19 PDS research guardrails: unequal family performance is acceptable; persistent dominance, persistent obsolescence, and hard exclusion are the failure conditions.

CP155 corrects the selector rather than changing the balance philosophy.

## Frozen authority and offensive anchor

CP155 makes no production C#, Concept, or active Technology Numerical Matrix change. It freezes:

- native-accepted CP153 main-weapon evidence, using K1/E7 defenders and GP M2/M3 plus Swarmer SW2 attackers;
- current Hull, Shield, Armor, DEF/RES, AUX, EW/Sensor, movement, missile cadence, PDS Space, and provisional Reactor/TP supply;
- CP147 tactical-utility combat doctrine;
- the current single-arriving-Flight research cadence.

The no-PDS CP153 relationship is an explicit anchor, not a defect to be equalized away. GP/Swarmer pressure against a K/E ship that declines PDS is intentionally dangerous. PDS purchases counterplay; it is not intended to erase missile identity or force every matchup to 50/50.

## Historical PDS evidence retained

CP155 treats the earlier Combat Model Lab v17-v19 PDS work, as registered by CP142, as supporting sensitivity evidence:

- Reaction Capacity changes are discrete architecture breakpoints.
- Chance × RC is a dominant interaction and must be evaluated conditionally rather than as independent knobs.
- Ammunition primarily becomes important at exhaustion/endurance boundaries once a practical magazine threshold is reached.
- The older research branch carried a late K RC2 progression and a late AMM range-1 third opportunity. Those are useful priors, not automatic numerical restorations.

CP154 remains the current-engine broad response surface; v17-v19 supplements it rather than replacing it.

## Corrected selection philosophy

CP155 has a hard prohibition on both:

1. a global distance-to-50-percent PDS selection objective; and
2. minimizing inter-family win-rate variance as a proxy for balance.

K-PDS, E-PDS, and AMM are allowed to lead different TLs, attackers, strata, and resource environments. Temporary superiority and meaningful swings are desirable when they produce rational choices. The final analysis is Pareto/viability oriented and reports persistent-dominance warnings rather than manufacturing equality.

Missile-armed defenders with PDS remain in the deep study only as **secondary robustness diagnostics**. They cannot drive candidate selection.

## Targeted candidate design

CP155 tests **1,846 candidate-TL points**.

### Kinetic PDS — true boundary extension

CP154 showed that late natural RC2 remained strong even at its weakest tested chance. CP155 therefore extends only the unresolved boundary:

- base chance: 0 / 2 / 5 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 15 / 18 percentage points;
- RC1 or RC2;
- full readiness TP: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4;
- ammunition: **fixed 75**;
- same-hex terminal windows only; no range-1 or Strain architecture.

This deliberately brackets weak RC2 states far below CP154 without reopening unrelated K-PDS dimensions.

### Energy PDS — architecture rescoring

CP154 already mapped the broad Energy response surface, including Strain Limit 1-4. CP155 therefore focuses the doctrine-consistent states that need deep confirmation:

- base chance: 0 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 20;
- RC1 TP: 1 / 2 / 3;
- RC2 incremental TP: +1 / +2, so RC2 always costs more than RC1;
- RC1, **RC2_OVERCHARGED**, and **RC2_SAFE**;
- overcharged Strain Limit: 1 / 2 only;
- one Strain is added only when the extra overcharged reaction actually fires;
- ammunition-free; no range-1 capability.

The ladder architecture explicitly includes RC1 controls, Strain-1/2 overcharged-RC2 maturation, and trajectories that later make RC2 safe.

### AMM — architecture rescoring

CP154 already established that the unique range-1 opportunity is real and valuable. CP155 focuses on coherent readiness/unlock trajectories:

- base chance: 0 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 20;
- ammunition: **fixed 25**, deliberately exhaustible in an extended combat;
- RC1 TP 1/2;
- several RC2 and RC3 tiered TP profiles;
- RC3 candidates from TL5 onward;
- **RC3 always and only means one range-1 pre-terminal opportunity plus two terminal opportunities**.

K and E cannot acquire RC3 or range 1.

## Ammunition policy

Ammunition is intentionally removed as a fine balance axis in CP155.

- K-PDS uses 75 rounds throughout the tested ladder: an endurance allowance intended to support multiple ordinary combats before logistics/reload matters.
- AMM uses 25 interceptors throughout: enough to be useful while permitting meaningful exhaustion during a long engagement.
- Energy remains ammunition-free.

Future campaign logistics may alter those values, but arbitrary per-TL magazine optimization is prohibited in this pass.

## Hard technology constraints

Whole-ladder candidates must be valid technology histories before scoring:

- base interception chance cannot regress;
- RC cannot regress;
- unlocked capabilities cannot disappear;
- quiet TLs are allowed;
- within the same capability state, readiness TP may hold or improve but not worsen;
- a genuine capability jump may require additional TP;
- K RC2, once unlocked, remains unlocked;
- Energy may progress RC1 → overcharged RC2 → safe RC2, but safe RC2 cannot regress to strain-requiring RC2;
- Energy overcharged Strain Limit may hold or mature 1 → 2, not regress;
- AMM RC3/range-1, once unlocked, remains available;
- K ammo remains 75 and AMM ammo remains 25.

The selector therefore chooses among believable TL1-TL9 technology ladders rather than nine unrelated local winners.

## Primary screen and no-PDS anchor

The primary selection surface contains only K1/E7 defenders versus GP M2, GP M3, and Swarmer SW2 where unlocked, crossed with six accepted combat strata and all five resource environments.

This yields **1,560 primary contexts**.

A separate 200-trial no-PDS run over those exact contexts provides the offensive anchor:

- **312,000 combats**.

The PDS candidate screen runs 30 trials/cell over **320,640 candidate-context cells**:

- **9,619,200 combats**.

Candidate outputs report absolute protection uplift relative to the no-PDS reference, with GP and Swarmer retained separately. No global equality target is computed.

## Technology-stratified ladder synthesis

CP155 synthesizes **10 whole-TL ladders per family** from tested candidates only. Architecture templates force representation rather than allowing a scalar score to prune the interesting mechanisms:

- K: RC1 controls plus multiple late RC2 unlock trajectories;
- E: RC1 controls, Strain-1 and Strain-2 overcharged RC2, and overcharged→safe maturation;
- AMM: RC1/RC2 controls plus multiple RC3/range-1 unlock trajectories.

Low/mid/high empirical protection-uplift points remain eligible within each architecture. Deep confirmation therefore observes the trade space rather than preselecting a desired outcome.

## Deep confirmation and viability map

All **30 ladders** receive:

- the full 1,560-context primary surface; plus
- **300 secondary robustness contexts** using missile-armed defenders only at the central resource environment.

That is **1,860 contexts per ladder** × 100 trials/cell:

- **5,580,000 deep combats**.

The deep merger evaluates all **10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000** K/E/AMM triad combinations offline. It reports leadership counts, worst deficits, long disadvantage streaks, and persistent-dominance watches. It deliberately exports **no triad equalization score** and does not invent an unmeasured interaction among three PDS installations.

Total substantive CP155 scale is:

- no-PDS anchor: 312,000;
- focused screen: 9,619,200;
- deep confirmation: 5,580,000;
- **total: 15,511,200 combats**.

## Intended decision

CP155 is intended to answer the remaining PDS design questions without another broad PDS sweep:

- whether and when K can gain natural RC2 once its lower effectiveness boundary is properly bracketed;
- whether Energy's unique TP/Strain RC2 should remain overcharged for part of the ladder and when/if it matures into safe RC2;
- whether Strain Limit 1 or 2 best preserves overcharge as a reserved burst capability;
- when AMM should gain RC2 and the unique RC3/range-1 architecture;
- whether K/E/AMM trade leadership and retain rational niches across TLs, GP/Swarmer matchups, strata, and TP environments without persistent family-wide dominance or obsolescence.

No result automatically promotes production numbers or mechanics. If PDS closes, the intended next balance layer remains the rest of the Shield/Armor/AUX lifetime-value sweep, followed by final whole-ship Reactor/TP scarcity tuning.
