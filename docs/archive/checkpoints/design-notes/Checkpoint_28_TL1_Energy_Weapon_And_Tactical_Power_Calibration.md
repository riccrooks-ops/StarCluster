# Checkpoint 28 - TL1 Energy Weapon and Tactical Power Calibration

Checkpoint 28 extends the accepted Checkpoint 27c foundation with a 31-variant TL1 energy study. It adds a generalized energy/kinetic duel simulator, low/standard/two-shot-safe-burst energy doctrines, Tactical Power and tactical Shield-recharge accounting, degraded reactor and computer variants, range-retention controls, cross-family side swaps, ten focused tests, and complete output reporting.

Forced energy-weapon overload beyond Strain Limit remains deferred. No accepted Checkpoint 27c kinetic or defensive value is changed.
