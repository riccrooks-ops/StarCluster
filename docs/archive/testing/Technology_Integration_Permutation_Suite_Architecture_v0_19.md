# Technology Integration Permutation Suite Architecture v0.19

Checkpoint 104 is a **bounded diagnostic-closure pass**, not another broad TL3 calibration cycle. Native-accepted CP103 remains the substantive TL1/TL2/TL3 population evidence and the accepted research-simulation architecture. CP104 asks four questions exposed by CP103 and then deliberately hands the project back to technology-chart expansion.

## Accepted foundation

- CP99 v0.8 remains the frozen TL1/TL2 exact-edge regression consumer.
- CP102 v0.9/v1.0 remain the native-accepted executable TL3 construction/transition regression consumers.
- CP103 v1.1 is native-accepted population screening evidence: 921,600 raw -> 164,160 legal builds, 96 cells, 1,152 variants, 288,000 substantive trials.
- CP103 v1.2 is native-accepted zero-weight all-tier diagnostic evidence: 28 materialized legal builds, 100 variants, 25,000 substantive trials.
- CPython 3.13 stdlib-only research simulation remains screening authority; native C#/Godot mechanics/tests remain gameplay authority.

## CP104 targeted diagnostic closure — v1.3

`cross-tl-build-permutation-foundation-v1_3.json`

CP104 materializes **52 unique named legal builds** from a declared 124,002,900-state option contract. It defines 128 mirrored logical pairings and two Adaptive Engage movement-order geometries for **256 variants**. At 500 trials per variant the new substantive workload is **128,000 trials**. Population weight is always zero.

The four causal questions are:

1. **Legacy-main response:** Can intelligently filled contemporary K3/E3 ships answer exact-fill dual K1/K2 or E1/E2 designs when the frontier ship actually spends its available Space rather than remaining at the CP103 underfilled 30/36 control?
2. **Mover-order decomposition:** For complete Kinetic and Missile TL2->TL3 packages, is CP103's mover-order cliff primarily STL3, the non-STL TL3 package, or the interaction between them?
3. **Energy synergy decomposition:** Which TL3 Energy elements create the robust complete-tier advantage—E3 itself, Sensor3, Reactor3, Computer3, their combinations, or the wider complete package?
4. **Power/population sensitivity:** Which one-TP changes remove the CP103 power-starvation hot spots, and how sensitive CP103's 57.96% population-weighted advanced-side result is to alternative non-player-prior weighting schemes?

CP104 analysis must preserve mover-order-first/second results, challenger/baseline power-shortfall telemetry, and the accepted CP103 statistical bundles used for reweighting sensitivity.

## Hard boundary on TL3 tuning

CP104 cannot automatically change a TL3 value. A balance preference, a large matchup swing, or an exploitable but opportunity-cost-bearing build is **not** by itself an architectural defect.

After CP104, the default project direction is:

> **extend the basic subsystem Technology-Level chart beyond TL3 and then test broader cross-TL interactions.**

Additional TL3-focused calibration is justified only if CP104 exposes a concrete mechanics/architecture defect that would invalidate higher-TL work—for example an impossible construction rule, a broken power-accounting invariant, a research/game parity failure, or an initiative artifact so severe that the standing cross-TL simulator cannot be interpreted.

## Population-weight sensitivity

CP103's combinatorial population is not a player-build prior. CP104 therefore reports the accepted CP103 statistical bundles under several review-only reweightings: original combinatorial population, equal statistical bundle, equal population cell, equal composition class, equal progression stratum, and equal Space-pair stratum. These are sensitivity lenses, not replacement priors.

## Acceptance boundary

Passing CP104 means the targeted v1.3 diagnostics execute reproducibly with zero trial errors and the CP103 reweight source is reproduced exactly. It does **not** mean a particular TL3 response is balanced, and it does not authorize further TL3 tuning by default.
