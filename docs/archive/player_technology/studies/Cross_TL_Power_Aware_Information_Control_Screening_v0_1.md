# Cross-TL Power-Aware Information-Control Screening v0.1

## Purpose

This is the Checkpoint 88 executable integration study definition. It strengthens the CP87 cross-TL foundation without promoting new gameplay values.

The study preserves the 512-build exact-fill TL1/TL2 working envelope, then expands the bounded representative combat slice from 64 to 96 ordered logical pairings. The original 64 complete/mixed pairings remain, while 32 additional pairings isolate the four information-control axes (Tactical Computer, Sensor, ECM, ECCM) across all 16 TL1/TL2 combinations at the accepted 6-TP reactor reference against TL1 Kinetic and contemporary TL2 Energy anchors. Three geometry/order contexts produce 288 generated variants.

## Durable controls

- Construction enumeration requires at least one Main Weapon and at least one Reactor. Optional explicit duplicates are not prohibited by the minimum guard.
- Simultaneous Tactical Power sufficiency is not a construction-legality filter.
- Generated combat uses the accepted preserve-combat-package EW doctrine. ECM/ECCM affordability uses actual rating-scaled power cost rather than base-cost placeholders.
- Dynamic contexts use TrackAwareOpponentRange.
- Intended-active fixed references must convert nonzero attack opportunities into actual attacks; dynamic contexts must preserve attack types active in the healthy fixed reference.
- Outcome and win-rate results are review evidence. No candidate is automatically promoted, demoted, or retuned.

## Information-control attribution slice

Side A holds Kinetic TL1 penetration, Reactor 6, Shield 2, and AP0/AI4 while permuting Computer 10/12, Sensor DR0/DR1, ECM1/2, and ECCM1/2. Each of the 16 combinations faces both a TL1 Kinetic anchor and the current TL2 Energy working build. This isolates information-control composition while retaining a contemporary power budget and a stress opponent that previously exposed self-starvation.

The slice is diagnostic. It does not imply that all four TL2 information-control properties must remain, be reduced, or progress together.
