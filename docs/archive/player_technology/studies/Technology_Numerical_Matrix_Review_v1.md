# Technology Numerical Matrix Review v1

**Checkpoint:** 109 candidate architecture  
**Status:** whole-ladder numerical candidate review; not calibrated, not promoted, not wired into production runtime.

## Purpose

Checkpoint 109 translates the native-accepted CP108 qualitative Technology Family Storyboard/table into the first complete candidate numerical TL1-TL9 matrix. The goal is a coherent surface for later testing, not balance certification. Earlier TL1-TL3 values are evidence seeds and executable-history references, not locks on the new whole-ladder cadence.

## Authority and evidence stack

1. Active Concept v0.7i design rules and the CP108 qualitative architecture.
2. Technology Family Storyboard v1.2: 10 disciplines, 32 lineages, 214 beats.
3. Technology Component Table v0.2 and CP108 qualitative review: default/branch ownership and player-expression classes.
4. Technology Idea Register and Foundation Completeness Audit.
5. Preserved reference-mining synthesis, especially qualitative progression, architectural breakthroughs, family identity, sparse causal prerequisites, missile-axis separation, information ecology, and power/storage separation.
6. Existing TL1-TL3 runtime/candidate profiles as empirical/mechanical evidence, but not as immutable progression spacing.

## Review rules used

- A new operating principle may create a larger adjacent-TL jump than a maturation step.
- No family receives a number merely because a TL cell exists. Quiet steps and holds are legal.
- Maturation may improve Space, efficiency, damaged behavior, readiness, integration, safe operating modes, or reliability instead of raw output.
- A successor family need not erase a mature predecessor. Early successor technology can be stronger on one frontier and weaker in resilience/Space/TP burden.
- Weapon families retain distinct progression axes; damage/range/penetration do not all rise together.
- Missile delivery, guidance/seeker, payload, and AMM defense remain separable.
- Sensor/EW advancement may improve reach, discrimination, efficiency, and information robustness without becoming omniscient.
- Hard external prerequisites are sparse, causal, normally one prerequisite in this candidate, and unlock automatically when the owning TL and final prerequisite are both known.
- Tall research retains meaningful ungated vertical progression; breadth adds cross-pollinated options rather than becoming mandatory.
- Existing production/runtime values remain C#/Godot data until a later checkpoint deliberately promotes replacements.

## Major whole-ladder conclusions

### Power

The old incremental TL2 Power seed is intentionally revised. Peak Fission at TL1 remains 5 Operational TP / 6 Space. Early Practical Fusion at TL2 moves the output frontier to a provisional 7 TP but is brittle when damaged (3 Degraded / 0 Emergency). Mature Compact Fusion at TL3 retains 7 peak TP, falls to 5 Space, and improves damaged-state resilience to 4/1. High-Output Fusion pushes to 9 TP at TL4. Early Antimatter creates the next large frontier at TL5 (12 TP) but again begins brittle; TL6 matures the family through compactness/resilience rather than more peak output. TL8 repeats the pattern for early matter conversion before the mature TL9 endpoint.

This deliberately creates uneven jumps at the family handoffs rather than a universal +1 TP per TL rule.

### Propulsion

Sublight Move grows nonuniformly: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 6, 7, 9, 10. TL5 and TL8 are deliberate frontier jumps associated with antimatter-catalyzed and inertial-coupled principles; TL6 is a maturation step that holds Move. Fuel remains 2 per traversed hex pending the Resources/endurance pass. Strategic FTL likewise permits qualitative/geometry improvements without mandatory Move growth (TL5 holds raw Strategic Move while transition flexibility improves).

### Hull and Installation Space

Hull capacity grows cautiously from 35 Space at TL1-TL2 to 45 at TL9, while integrated systems also miniaturize. The candidate whole-ship sanity sheet therefore shows increasing discretionary design freedom at high TL. This is intentional as a first architecture candidate but is a major calibration target: high-TL hull capacity plus subsystem miniaturization must not make all meaningful choices disappear.

### Armor and Shields

Passive Armor alternates Impact and Penetration improvement through TL6, then deliberately holds at TL7-TL8 while powered/reactive and field-assisted protection appear as optional branches. TL9 programmable matter resumes the passive-material frontier. Shields progress primarily through capacity/recharge/efficiency while specialist hardening, particle screening, and anti-penetration stabilization remain separate components.

### Sensors / EW and Computing

The matrix uses the later Balanced-0 TL1 sensor authority (Passive Firm/Approximate 1/3; Active 3/4; overload 4/5), not stale early fixtures. TL3 converts the former overload-scale 4/5 envelope into a safe High Active mode. Later levels widen range/discrimination and periodically improve ECM/ECCM efficiency instead of escalating rating every TL. TL9 remains bounded by normal uncertainty and the map's finite 10-hex maximum separation.

Computing deliberately combines modest direct-targeting growth with degraded-operation recovery, Approximate-track penalty reduction, Evasive Compensation, and later Space reduction. It does not become a universal combat multiplier or hidden-state oracle.

### Projectile Weapons

TL2 improved penetrator materials belong to the ammunition lineage, not a fictional new accelerator. The representative TL2 kinetic attack therefore uses the TL1 mass driver with APEN 1 ammunition. Kinetic PDS is deliberately quiet at TL2: the old Base Chance 13 / Ammo 60 bump moves to TL3 because the Storyboard contains no independent TL2 Kinetic-PDS beat. Later helical, dense-penetrator, micro-submunition, macron, and field-assisted concepts remain branches where appropriate rather than silently replacing the standard accelerator.

### Energy Weapons

TL2 explicitly expresses improved optics/pulse conditioning through range/accuracy, not damage inflation. TL3 makes the high-output mode safe. Later coherent-beam improvements alternate range, packet output, penetration, power burden, and Space; free-electron/tunable, ion/particle, plasma, and extreme-frequency weapons remain branches. The TL9 coherent-energy lance remains a beam-family pinnacle rather than inheriting matter-conversion damage merely because TL9 Power exists.

### Missiles and AMM

TL2 improves missile propulsion/endurance without silently upgrading guidance or payload. TL3 adds onboard navigation; TL4 adds the first normal terminal seeker; TL5 adds cooperative seeker behavior while the launcher/flight profile may hold; TL6 and TL7 push sprint propulsion; TL8 advances seeker quality while field-assisted terminal maneuver remains an optional gated branch; TL9 remains an on-map, interceptable field-coupled strike vehicle. Warheads are separate payload variants. Local AMM and extended-range AMM remain distinct lineages/roles.

## Sparse hard-prerequisite candidate set

The v0.1 matrix contains 16 hard-prerequisite relationships across 214 Storyboard beats. Only four are on standard progression; the rest are branch/cross-pollinated technologies. Every candidate currently uses one external prerequisite, below the design ceiling of one or occasionally two.

Standard-gated examples are intentionally causal: Fusion-assisted STL requires practical Fusion knowledge; Antimatter-catalyzed/plasma STL requires Antimatter Power knowledge; the TL7 Antimatter missile drive requires Antimatter Power. The owning discipline retains other native advances at those TL regions, so missing a gate does not freeze the whole research tree.

## Lower-TL reconciliation

The matrix explicitly revisits seven areas where old values were either stale or products of the former incremental ladder:

- Power TL2: 6 TP seed -> 7/3/0 TP Early Fusion candidate.
- Power TL3: 6/3/1 TP seed -> 7/4/1 TP Mature Compact Fusion candidate at 5 Space.
- Kinetic PDS TL2: old independent bump removed; TL1 mount held until TL3 maturation.
- Energy Main TL2: introduces range/accuracy expression for the Storyboard's optics/pulse beat.
- Missile Delivery TL2: introduces movement/range expression for propulsion/endurance.
- Sensor TL1: explicitly uses the later Balanced-0 1/3, 3/4, 4/5 architecture rather than stale early range fixtures.
- General TL1-TL3 policy: prior acceptance demonstrates mechanics and integration; it does not lock the progression story.

Historical/runtime profiles are retained in the repository for provenance. CP109 does not silently rewrite production values.

## Whole-ship Space sanity (candidate only)

The representative mandatory core is one Main Weapon, one Reactor, primary STL, primary FTL, Tactical Computer, and Sensor. It consumes 28/35 Space at TL1-TL2, 27/36 at TL3, 27/37 at TL4, 27/38 at TL5, 24/39 at TL6, 22/41 at TL7, 21/43 at TL8, and 20/45 at TL9. A representative two-Main/two-Reactor core first fits exactly at TL5 before optional systems.

These are deterministic construction checks, not balance claims. The rapid growth in high-TL discretionary Space is intentionally flagged for later integration calibration rather than suppressed before the full matrix exists.

## Calibration boundary

No combat simulation, Monte Carlo sweep, research-engine study, or promotion test belongs in CP109. The matrix is meant to exist as one coherent candidate surface first. Later checkpoints should calibrate controlled subsystem slices while continuously exercising mixed-TL, nonadjacent-TL, Space/power, legacy-technology, branch, prerequisite, and whole-ship interactions against this complete candidate environment.
