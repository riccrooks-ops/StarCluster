# Player TL1-TL9 Technology Architecture v0.10

## Purpose

Checkpoint 58 accepts Checkpoint 57a as a negative multi-main architecture result and opens TL4 as the first **single-main mid-tech subsystem generation**. TL1-TL3 remain frozen.

## Three-generation cadence

| Generation | Foundation | Refinement | Maturity | Main Weapon Capacity | AUX progression |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Low tech | TL1 | TL2 | TL3 | 1 throughout | 1 / 1 / 2 |
| Mid tech | TL4 | TL5 | TL6 | 1 throughout | 2 / 2 / 3 |
| High tech | TL7 | TL8 | TL9 | 1 throughout | 3 / 3 / 4 |

Standard player-cruiser Main Weapon capacity is `1 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 1`. AUX Capacity remains `1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 4`. Multi-main remains available only as future nonstandard-ship design space.

## Checkpoint 57a conclusion

The unrestricted TL4 second-main test is retained as accepted negative evidence. A naked TL4 dual-main ship broadly invalidated mature specialized TL3 ships. The response is to remove the extra standard-player main weapon, not to keep it and add an arbitrary fire-control prohibition.

## TL4 screening philosophy

TL4 is a genuine generational foundation. A roughly 75-80% conditional advantage over mature TL3 is a starting calibration region, not a hard gate. Mature TL3 specialization must still matter. Checkpoint 58 therefore isolates fire-control, weapon output/power cost, reactor output, structure, primary Armor Protection, shield capacity, and mobility before screening composed packages.

Higher-output weapon candidates add +1 DAM and +1 Tactical Power activation cost. This explicitly tests the principle that greater subsystem capability may create greater tactical-power demand. Reactor growth is measured separately and in combination.

## Powered defensive AUX entry

Shield Hardener and Energized Armor Controller enter runtime screening at TL4. Both consume sustained Tactical Power while active. Shield Hardener adds bounded Shield Protection. Energized Armor adds temporary primary Armor Protection; passive armor remains unchanged if the controller is unpowered or unavailable. Strength and power cost are screened independently with 1/1, 1/2, and 2/2 effect/power candidates.

One powered defensive AUX may be paired with the mature TL3 B4G1 Combat Battery or C2D1 Power Capacitor inside TL4's two-AUX budget. The Auxiliary Reactor remains a two-AUX sustained source and therefore cannot accompany another ordinary one-AUX system at TL4.

## Promotion policy

Checkpoint 58 is screening-only. No TL4 standard profile, Shield Hardener value, or Energized Armor value is promoted automatically.
