# Technology Family Storyboard v1

**Checkpoint 105 status:** architecture foundation. This document defines technology-family stories and candidate idea placement only. It does **not** change the numerical TL chart, accepted TL1-TL3 component values, combat mechanics, or simulation authority.

## 1. Core architecture

Star Cluster now treats the **technology family story** as the conceptual layer above component tables. A Technology Level describes knowledge within one discipline, but the meaning of a particular TL depends on the family being advanced. Blank or quiet TLs are valid. A mature legacy family may coexist with, outlive, or later reappear beside a successor.

### Era-tone guide (soft, not mandatory)

| TL | Broad tone | Architecture intent |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Slightly Futuristic Baseline | Highly mature late-near-future engineering plus the few setting-defining breakthroughs needed for Star Cluster, especially practical FTL and baseline defensive fields. TL1 should feel sophisticated rather than primitive. |
| 2 | Lower-SF Introduction | First broadly practical post-baseline breakthroughs. New principles may be awkward, bulky, power-hungry, or incomplete. |
| 3 | Lower-SF Integration | Many early breakthroughs become shipworthy, integrated, compact, reliable, or tactically flexible. TL3 is not a universal maturation tier; this is only a broad era tone. |
| 4 | Mature Lower-SF | Lower-SF families reach strong practical forms and begin creating new subsystem architectures rather than only scalar improvements. |
| 5 | Early Higher-SF | Technologies with severe real-world engineering barriers become credible player tools; mature lower-SF families may receive peak or specialist revivals. |
| 6 | Integrated Higher-SF | Advanced materials, computation, field control, autonomous engineering, particle technologies, and antimatter-class systems become more routinely integrated. |
| 7 | Mature Higher-SF | High-energy and highly automated systems reach strong operational forms. Weird-science side branches may appear, but should still have explicit costs and counters. |
| 8 | Early Science-Fantasy Edge | Player science begins manipulating matter, fields, signatures, inertia, and spacetime in ways that are deliberately beyond conservative engineering extrapolation. |
| 9 | Player-Technological Pinnacle | The normal player ladder reaches its strongest coherent science-fantasy forms. TL9 should be campaign-defining but remain bounded and distinct from Precursor rule-breaking. |

These bands are a **tone and plausibility guide**, not a universal unlock schedule. FTL and baseline Shields are deliberate setting miracles already present at TL1. A family may introduce a higher-SF idea early when the setting requires it, or retain grounded engineering deep into later TLs when that produces a better story.

### Lifecycle/status labels

- **base** - Mature starting-world technology or a later derivative/revival of that baseline family. Base does not mean obsolete or primitive.
- **existing** - Already expected or established by the current Star Cluster Concept/accepted architecture. Story placement may still be refined before numerical table work.
- **candidate** - A strong proposed addition with a coherent scientific/engineering premise and gameplay role. Candidate is not yet an adopted component or numerical TL-table entry.
- **deferred** - Worth preserving, but the owning family, era, prerequisites, gameplay role, or implementation cost is not settled enough for promotion.
- **exotic** - Precursor-grade, rule-bending, deliberately extreme weird science, or technology otherwise outside the normal player-developed TL1-TL9 ladder.

### Structural-role tags

- **core family** - Primary lineage normally represented by a standard/base component family.
- **maturation** - Improves integration, efficiency, reliability, packaging, resilience, or a bounded operating property without changing the core principle.
- **branch** - A specialist or alternate lineage that coexists with the main family rather than automatically replacing it.
- **cross pollinated derivative** - An owning-tree technology unlocked or transformed by a genuinely enabling external research discipline.
- **legacy revival** - A mature older family becomes strategically interesting again because later science enables a new niche or performance envelope.
- **operating capability** - A mode or capability applied to an existing family rather than a new hardware family.
- **specialist auxiliary** - A support installation that creates a meaningful design choice without becoming a new visible research tree.
- **one off** - A useful technology that may have no natural multi-TL progression and does not need a successor merely to fill a cell.
- **infrastructure** - A strategic/site technology rather than an ordinary player-cruiser component.
- **campaign technology** - A technology whose principal value is exploration, logistics, salvage, diplomacy, or campaign consequence rather than direct combat.
- **weird science** - A speculative but potentially normal-player scientific concept that can exist as a one-off or rare branch without becoming Precursor magic.
- **precursor artifact** - Non-player-developed Precursor technology or artifact behavior; TL10 is shorthand only.

## 2. Global design rules

- Technology families tell their own development story; no family is forced to populate every TL or follow identical breakpoints.
- TL1 is a highly mature slightly futuristic baseline, except for deliberate setting miracles such as practical FTL and baseline shields.
- TL2-4 broadly feel like lower science fiction, TL5-7 higher science fiction, and TL8-9 increasingly science fantasy; these are tonal guides, not hard gates.
- New families should move at least one useful capability/design frontier; mature legacy technologies may remain competitive through a real niche.
- An older family may receive a later revival when its owning field or an enabling external field creates a genuinely new derivative.
- Cross-pollination is sparse and causal. The owning research category remains explicit and external prerequisites are limited to genuinely enabling science.
- One-off and weird-science technologies are allowed. A concept does not need a multi-TL sequel to deserve preservation.
- Precursor/TL10 is shorthand only and remains outside normal player-developed TL1-TL9 research.
- This storyboard is conceptual architecture, not a numerical component table or automatic unlock list.

## 5. Precursor / “TL10” shorthand

TL10 remains **conversational shorthand for Precursor-grade technology**, not a normal research level. Precursor devices may violate one ordinary rule explicitly, depend on matched interfaces, or use scientific principles the player cannot reproduce. CP105 records these ideas so they are not lost; it does not make them researchable.

Examples captured in the Idea Register include singularity power/weapons, stable wormhole or impossible transit devices, phase cloaks/armor, causal sensors, dimensional storage, temporal repair and living Precursor ships.

## 6. What CP105 intentionally does not decide

- No Space, Tactical Power, range, damage, penetration, movement, recharge, capacity, reliability or other numerical values are assigned here.
- No CP104 technology value is promoted, demoted or retuned.
- Candidate/deferred story beats are not automatic component unlocks.
- No additional visible research category is created by this document. Branches such as particle/ion weapons, thermal support, gravity, stealth, storage, probes or transit infrastructure remain owned by an existing category unless a later design decision says otherwise.
- No simulation or calibration is required for this architecture checkpoint.
