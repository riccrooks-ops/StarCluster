# Cross-Pollination and Legacy Revival Map v1

**Checkpoint 105 status:** architecture foundation. This map identifies causal research relationships; it does not add blanket prerequisites or numerical bonuses.

## Rules

- Every cross-pollinated technology keeps **one owning research category**.
- External prerequisites are used only when the external science is genuinely enabling, normally one or two categories.
- Broad low-TL research never synthesizes a higher-TL capability by stacking generic synergies.
- A legacy revival should create a **real niche**, not simply make an old family secretly best-in-slot.
- Cross-pollination may unlock a component, auxiliary, operating mode, material, warhead, seeker, repair method or infrastructure technology. It need not create a new visible tree.

## High-value enabling relationships

| Enabling research | Example owning technologies affected | Architecture use |
|---|---|---|
| Armor / advanced materials | Hull structures; advanced fission containment; fusion/antimatter containment; radiators; coil/rail accelerators; beam optics; self-healing repair | Materials is the strongest broad enabler, but each derivative stays owned by its original subsystem tree. |
| Power | Energy/particle weapons; STL drives; shields; pulse storage; high-energy sensors; missile propulsion/warheads | Power prerequisites are reserved for science that truly needs the energy form/containment capability, not for generic gating. |
| Computing / Fire Control | Cooperative sensing; smart projectiles; seekers; adaptive EW; PDS coordination; Damage Control robotics; autonomous probes | Computing unlocks processing/coordination behavior, never hidden enemy information or free universal bonuses. |
| Sensors / EW | Anti-emitter missiles; cooperative fire control; adaptive shield/beam tuning; stealth counterplay; mine sweeping | Information systems can enable specialist counterplay without collapsing into Computing. |
| Propulsion | Missile motors; exploration probes; FTL navigation tools | Propulsion science may be reused at smaller scale while the Missile family remains the owner of its weapon application. |
| Hull | High-energy mount integration; distributed beam directors; thermal-service capacity; repair/fabrication | Hull can enable installation architecture without turning every high-TL component into a Hull prerequisite. |
| Shields | Field-assisted armor; particle screens; late field-guided weapons/propulsion | Use sparingly at higher TL where field science is genuinely the enabling principle. |

## Legacy revival candidates

| Legacy family | Later enabler | Revival concept | Why it can coexist with successors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peak Fission | Advanced materials + direct energy conversion | **Advanced/Optimum Fission specialist reactor** | Ruggedness, low maintenance, damage tolerance, fuel availability or benign containment can matter more than peak output. |
| Electric / nuclear-electric propulsion | Better power electronics, superconductors and materials | **Long-endurance expedition/probe drive** | Propellant efficiency/endurance remain valuable even when tactical drives use more exotic principles. |
| Nuclear thermal / fission pulse propulsion | High-temperature materials / structural integration | **Rugged sprint or expedition branch** | Mission-dependent thrust and propellant tradeoffs preserve a niche. |
| Mature Kinetic accelerators | Superconductors, smart ammunition, nanomaterials | **Modernized mass driver / guided projectile branch** | Low continuous power and finite physical ammunition remain distinct from Energy weapons. |
| Mature coherent lasers | Phased arrays, tunable emitters, better optics/computing | **Distributed/tunable beam branch** | A familiar beam family can gain flexibility/resilience without becoming a particle weapon. |
| Spin gravity | Better structures and controls | **Compact/stowable rotating habitat** | Cheap/rugged passive architecture may remain useful after generated gravity exists. |
| Command-guided missiles | Better datalinks/ECCM | **Cheap network-guided missile** | Low-cost missiles can remain attractive beside autonomous seekers when the launching ship can maintain a good track. |
| Kinetic PDS | Smart projectiles / better local fire control | **Efficient modernized terminal PDS** | Cheap finite-ammo local defense can coexist with Beam PDS and AMM layers. |

## One-off cross-pollinated technologies worth preserving

- Thermal-suppression module: Power + Sensors/EW.
- Particle/radiation hardening: Armor + Sensors/EW/Computing as needed.
- Anti-emitter missile seeker: Missile Weapons + Sensors/EW.
- Molecular repair fabricator: Hull + Armor/materials.
- Smart guided projectile: Projectile Weapons + Computing.
- Tunable-spectrum beam: Energy Weapons + Computing/Power.
- Generated-gravity habitat: Hull + Power; tactical effects deferred.
- Tractor/tow field: Shields/field science with Hull/salvage use.
- Fixed transit anchor: Propulsion + Power/Hull; infrastructure rather than normal ship equipment.

## Guardrail against “synergy soup”

The existence of these relationships does **not** imply a general Materials bonus, a global Computing bonus, or a requirement that every advanced component depend on several trees. The later table should record only prerequisites that explain a concrete capability that could not otherwise exist.
