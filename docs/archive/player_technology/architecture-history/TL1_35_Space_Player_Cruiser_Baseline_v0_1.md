# TL1 35-Space Player Cruiser Baseline v0.1

This is the working construction baseline for the revised Star Cluster technology pass. It is a concept/simulation seed, not a final all-TL equipment table.

## Space architecture

The TL1 player cruiser has **35 Installation Spaces**. The mandatory primary architecture is one 6-Space main weapon, at least one 6-Space main reactor, one 5-Space STL drive, one 5-Space FTL drive, and one 3-Space tactical computer/fire-control architecture: **25 mandatory Space**, leaving 10 Space for specialization.

Primary STL, FTL, and tactical computer systems are one ship-wide architecture each and are not duplicated as full copies. Later Auxiliary systems may provide limited backup computer or STL capability, but not FTL. Main weapons and reactors are naturally duplicable when the full Space/power/opportunity cost is paid.

Base primary armor is hull-integrated/external and consumes **0 Installation Space**. Optional armor enhancements may consume Auxiliary/support Space.

## Working TL1 footprint

| System | Space | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Main weapon | 6 | Mandatory at least one; duplicable |
| Main reactor | 6 | Mandatory at least one; duplicable |
| STL drive | 5 | One primary architecture; ordinary floor expected near 4-5 |
| FTL drive | 5 | Mandatory for player; one primary architecture; no backup FTL |
| Tactical computer/fire control | 3 | One primary architecture |
| Active sensor suite | 3 | Optional; passive/navigation sensing remains core capability |
| Shield generator | 3 | Optional |
| Kinetic PDS | 2 | Optional; duplicable if later rules allow |
| Auxiliary/PDS ammunition support | 1 | Provisional pending magazine review |
| Small AUX | 1 | Useful lower granularity for batteries, capacitors, adapters, etc. |

The balanced reference concept uses all 35 Space. A dual-main striker also fits at 35 by sacrificing defenses. Two main weapons + two main reactors + both drives + the computer require 37 Space and therefore do not fit at TL1; later miniaturization or bounded Hull-TL growth may unlock that configuration naturally.

## Mechanical seed

Do not invent a new TL1 combat model for this architecture. Reuse the previously validated TL1 relationships as the numerical/mechanical seed: Hull 12; AI 4/AP 0; Shield 2 with base recharge 1 and tactical recharge; 5/3/1/0 main-reactor Tactical Power by condition; established weapon-family identities and resource costs; existing PDS, sensors/EW, Damage Control, component-condition, ammunition, battery/capacitor, and internal-critical behavior. Remap those mechanics into Installation Space and revalidate normal and odd legal builds.

Do **not** carry forward the obsolete Weapon Bay/AUX-capacity architecture, a blanket single-main rule, or fixed TL-versus-TL win targets.

## Progression tests

Every change must pass both a logical check and a mathematical/combinatorial check. New technology should expand a meaningful capability frontier; mature legacy technology may retain useful efficiency, resilience, or integration niches. Enumerate contemporary combinations—including multiple compact legacy components—under the same Space, Tactical Power, and prerequisite constraints to detect Pareto domination and integer-breakpoint exploits.
