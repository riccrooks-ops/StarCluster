# Technology Foundation Completeness Audit v1.2

**Status:** Foundation remains complete after qualitative technology-table review; no balance calibration

Confirm that the broader player-ship and campaign foundation remains complete after CP108 qualitative review of the TL1-TL9 technology/component table, while preserving KISS abstraction boundaries and the no-numerical-calibration boundary.

## Foundation guardrails

- Completeness does not mean simulation density. Existing abstractions remain preferred unless a new player decision earns a separate mechanic.
- The support/AUX KISS rule remains in force: installed support exists only when it changes capability, risk, or efficiency in a real gameplay loop.
- The review adds no new foundation domain and no new numerical TL4-TL9 values.
