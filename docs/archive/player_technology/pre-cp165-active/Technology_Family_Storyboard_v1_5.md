# Technology Family Storyboard v1.5

**Checkpoint:** 123  
**Status:** whole-system reference baseline reconciled; no balance calibration

The Storyboard is the conceptual progression authority. Each beat is represented exactly once in Technology Component Table v0.5. Numerical characteristics are linked where mature enough; quiet TLs and qualitative/capability beats remain valid.
