# Weapon Family Simplification and Swarmer Architecture v0.2

**Checkpoint:** 123

CP123 preserves the KISS weapon-family rules from v0.1 and reconciles the newer numerical baseline. Normal strict Kinetic projectile improvements auto-mature on compatible launchers; the player does not select a per-shot "smart round." Swarmer is a distinct TL2+ Missile Flight family because its packet/coverage/PDS-saturation behavior is meaningfully different from the GP Flight.

Reference Swarmer maturation: TL2 2×D4/+10 guidance/-10 pp PDS; TL3 2×D5/+10/-10; TL5 2×D7/+10/-10; TL7+ 2×D8/+15/-15. It remains one counter, one terminal roll, Firm-terminal dependent, SPEN0/APEN0, and does not create extra critical rolls.
