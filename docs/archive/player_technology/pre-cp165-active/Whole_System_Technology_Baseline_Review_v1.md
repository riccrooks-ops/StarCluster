**Checkpoint:** 123  
**Accepted implementation baseline:** CP122 Corrected Replacement 1  
**Purpose:** establish a coherent TL1-TL9 reference hypothesis for future testing; no balance proof

## Structural reconciliation

The prior v0.4 table had 214 rows but only 211 unique Storyboard keys because three duplicates displaced real beats. CP123 rebuilds the table directly from Storyboard v1.5 and restores **Adaptive reactive armor architecture**, **Nuclear-pumped sacrificial laser**, and **Improved local AMM guidance**. The new authority has exactly **218 unique Storyboard beats and 218 unique Tech Table entries** across 10 disciplines and 33 lineages.

## Numerical baseline decisions

- **Hull:** canonical Hull 24,24,25,26,27,28,30,32,34 with Installation Space 35→45.
- **Damage Control:** TL1 70/50/40 and 1 Hull/kit; TL3 75/55/45; TL7 80/60/50 and 2 Hull/kit; TL9 85/65/55 and 3 Hull/kit. Optional drones/fabricators remain separate.
- **Armor/Shields:** use the finer x2 ruler selectively rather than forcing a full old-point jump.
- **Kinetic:** smart/maneuvering projectile maturity appears as automatic +5 ACC at TL4 and +10 from TL1 by TL6; APEN remains the stronger late identity.
- **Energy:** coherent-beam progression leans more Shield-facing; APEN grows more slowly than Kinetic.
- **Missile:** delivery, guidance, GP yield and Swarmer are separate profiles. GP yield is D10→D11→D13→D15. Swarmer is a distinct TL2+ two-packet Flight family.
- **Held after review:** Reactor, STL, FTL, Computer, Sensor, ECM/ECCM and PDS numerical spines.

## Technology-era context for testing

For progression analysis, use the project's broad discovery-era framing as context rather than as a rigid per-lineage rule:

- **TL1:** baseline technology;
- **TL2-TL4:** low-technology discoveries and maturation;
- **TL5-TL7:** mid-technology discoveries and maturation;
- **TL8-TL9:** high-technology discoveries/maturation.

The broad boundaries make TL1->2, TL4->5 and TL7->8 especially important attribution points, but individual technologies may appear, mature, hold, or age out on a different cadence. An unexpectedly large interior transition such as TL5->6 should be decomposed into same-composition performance and newly enabled build-envelope effects before the table is retuned.

**Swarmer lifecycle:** the standard Swarmer line has explicit maturation beats at TL2, TL3, TL5 and TL7. The TL7 Autonomous Distributed Swarmer is carried forward at TL8-TL9 as mature legacy technology. That carry-forward does **not** create a design requirement that Swarmer remain peer-optimal against every high-tech defense or receive automatic TL8/TL9 packet upgrades. Late performance should be judged by retained niche/utility, especially against PDS, and any high-tech descendant should require an independent technology-fiction justification.

## Explicit non-decisions

CP123 does not claim balance, add a scenario, run Monte Carlo, change production C#/Godot, change simulation mechanics, migrate critical cadence, or production-promote the revised table. It is the baseline to test next.
