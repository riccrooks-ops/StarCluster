# Star Cluster Candidate TL1-TL9 Numerical Technology Matrix v0.1

**Status:** provisional whole-ladder candidate; no calibration and no promotion.

This table is the numerical companion to the native-accepted CP108 qualitative architecture. Prior TL1-TL3 values are treated as evidence seeds rather than locks. The Storyboard may therefore justify a major jump, a quiet TL, a family handoff, or a maturation step that changes Space/efficiency instead of raw performance.

## Guardrails

- Storyboard progression is authoritative over old uniform TL cadence.
- Technology leaps may be uneven; new operating principles should move a capability/design frontier.
- Maturation may improve Space, efficiency, damaged behavior, integration, modes or reliability without raising a headline stat.
- Quiet TLs are valid and are represented as holds rather than filler upgrades.
- Legacy families may remain useful through resilience, compactness, efficiency or mission niche.
- Sparse hard prerequisites unlock automatically when the owning TL and final prerequisite TL are both satisfied; no second research project or unlock cost exists.
- Tall play retains meaningful native vertical progression; breadth opens additional cross-pollinated options rather than becoming a blanket requirement.
- Fuel remains 100 capacity / 2 per traversed hex / +1 EvM per turn pending the Resources/endurance economy pass.
