## Reviewed and held numerical spines

Reactor, STL, FTL, Computer, Sensor, ECM, ECCM, Kinetic PDS, Energy PDS, and AMM PDS retain their CP122 v0.2 rows exactly after whole-system review. Holding a mature profile is a deliberate result, not missing work.

- No new scenario is added by CP123.

- Historical checkpoint inputs remain frozen.
- Critical/H-X cadence remains deferred.
- Production TL1 Damage Control remains 1 canonical Hull per successful Repair Kit.
