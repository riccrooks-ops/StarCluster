# Cross-Source Themes - Expanded Spacedock Corpus

**Status:** reference-mining synthesis only. These themes organize candidate discussion; they are **not Star Cluster rules** or automatic technology promotions.

Checkpoint 92 extends the same CP91 synthesis layer after completing the preserved 24-source queue. Duplicate standalone videos are not counted as independent evidence; their refinements remain owned by `SD-SW`.

## RM-THEME-001 - Qualitative technology progression

**Sources:** broad corpus, especially SD-EW, SD-SW, SD-Q09, SD-Q10, SD-Q15

Repeatedly, higher technology changes **how a capability works**, not merely its scalar value: repair automation/self-repair; beam tuning, pulsing or steering; different accelerator architectures and ammunition; storage versus generation; multimodal sensing; improved seeker discrimination; thermal integration; and more flexible FTL topology.

**Candidate design principle:** prefer progression that unlocks new operating choices, design envelopes, modes, integrations or counters when fiction/gameplay support them. Existing Technology Architecture guardrails remain authoritative.

## RM-THEME-002 - Architectural technology breakthroughs

**Sources:** SD-NL, SD-EW, SD-SM, SD-SW, SD-Q10, SD-Q15, SD-Q23

Some discoveries change what ships or infrastructure are practical to build: reactor-coupled emitters, distributed beam directors, advanced thermal support, new storage architectures, biological support interfaces, or FTL gates/conduits.

**Candidate use:** distinguish maturation of an existing component from a breakthrough that unlocks a new installation/infrastructure architecture. Preserve useful legacy alternatives when plausible.

## RM-THEME-003 - Preserve weapon-family identity

**Sources:** SD-EW, SD-SM, SD-SW and all reviewed weapon chapters

Kinetic, Energy/Beam, Missile and defensive systems have different natural resources, engineering problems and progression axes. Scientific categories can blur; gameplay families should follow coherent technology lineage and behavior rather than literal matter-versus-energy taxonomy.

**Candidate use:** continue family-specific progression. Particle beams merit distinct mechanical study but current mining discussion does not require a new player-facing family; they may fit an Energy/Beam lineage unless future gameplay evidence proves otherwise.

## RM-THEME-004 - Engagement envelope is a systems interaction

**Sources:** SD-EW, SD-SM, SD-SW, SD-Q09, SD-Q12

Physical reach, useful damage concentration, target motion, pointing uncertainty, search/track quality, stealth, EW, missile guidance, closing geometry and interception all contribute to whether an attack can actually occur effectively.

**Current relationship:** strongly corroborates the existing separation among physical weapon capability, sensing/track quality and attack eligibility. No new range rule is created here.

## RM-THEME-005 - Cross-category interactions should be sparse and causal

**Sources:** SD-NL, SD-EW, SD-SW, SD-Q09, SD-Q10, SD-Q15

Examples include Computing enabling precision/timing, Power enabling particular emitter or storage architectures, thermal capability enabling unusually demanding components, and microfusion/nuclear payload science cross-pollinating Kinetic/Missile/Propulsion applications.

**Candidate use:** use explicit prerequisite/capability tags only where one subsystem genuinely enables another; avoid generic synergy bonuses or blanket vertical prerequisites.

## RM-THEME-006 - Specialized counterplay is preferable to universal nerfs

**Sources:** SD-SM, SD-SW, SD-Q09, SD-Q12, SD-Q15

Examples include anti-emitter weapons, decoys, radiation hardening, particle-specific screens, mine sweeping, AMMs, thermal-signature management and specialized low-observability counters.

**Candidate use:** when a strong capability creates a cliff, first ask whether a specialist counter or different operating choice creates better play than flattening the capability everywhere.

## RM-THEME-007 - Model engineering consequences, not unnecessary simulation detail

**Sources:** broad corpus

The references contain rich detail about radiators, recoil, barrel wear, radiation dose, food/water, sensor modalities, accelerator geometry and FTL physics. Most are better used as rationale for bounded game abstractions than as new bars or inventories.

**Candidate design principle:** add a tactical state/resource only when it creates a meaningful player decision not already represented by Installation Space, Tactical Power, Strain, condition, Ammo, fuel, track quality, broad resources or another existing abstraction.

## RM-THEME-008 - Rare weapons should be events

**Sources:** SD-NL, SD-SW

Bomb-pumped/sacrificial systems and superweapon examples suggest that exceptional weapons are memorable when they demand preparation, commitment and consequences.

**Candidate use:** future rare/Precursor/experimental weapons should consider charge time, conspicuous preparation, power commitment, Strain, one-shot resources or post-fire consequences rather than only very large DAM. A singular random Precursor weapon is a particularly natural place for this design language.

## RM-THEME-009 - Information states are a reusable gameplay resource

**Sources:** SD-SW, SD-Q09, SD-Q12, SD-Q23, SD-Q24

Sensors, stealth, minefields and FTL exploration all benefit from separating existence/suspicion, localization, weapons-quality tracking, identification and route knowledge. Exact hidden ratings need not be exposed for information itself to be useful.

**Candidate use:** preserve observer-safe graded knowledge: suspected hazard/contact, characterized region, Firm position, identified capability or mapped transit link are different kinds of knowledge.

## RM-THEME-010 - Missile delivery, guidance and payload should remain separable

**Sources:** SD-SW, especially reviewed Missile and Nuclear chapters

Missile propulsion/endurance, launcher/datalink support, onboard navigation/sensing, terminal seeker behavior and warhead effect mature along different axes. Unguided rockets remain a legitimate capability combination.

**Candidate use:** future Missile progression should not make a better seeker automatically invent a better warhead or vice versa. Missile Flight remains the attack-package abstraction while terminal payloads can differ.

## RM-THEME-011 - Resource identity and effective Ammo are abstraction tools

**Sources:** SD-SW and the project discussion prompted by Point Defense/Macron review

A player-facing Ammo point represents a useful attack/intercept package, not a literal bullet. High projectile count, improved packing, better guidance or more accurate defensive fire can therefore increase the number of meaningful engagements supported without requiring fractional per-shot costs.

**Project-discussion candidate (not a source observation, not adopted):** higher-TL PDS may sometimes gain a larger effective **Ammo** value to represent improved accuracy/efficiency while each PDS engagement continues to consume exactly 1 Ammo. This should be compared with genuine magazine-capacity increases during later PDS design.

## RM-THEME-012 - Power, storage and thermal support are related but distinct

**Sources:** SD-Q10, SD-Q15, SD-SW

Generation supplies sustained energy; storage handles peaks/time mismatch; thermal systems reject or temporarily store waste heat. Higher capability can trade output, storage rate, compactness, vulnerability, efficiency and support requirements.

**Candidate use:** keep routine support abstracted inside complete component Space/performance. Exceptional batteries, overload, thermal suppression or specialist support can become explicit only when they create a real choice.

## RM-THEME-013 - Sensors, EW and stealth form an information ecology

**Sources:** SD-SW, SD-Q09, SD-Q12, SD-Q15

Passive/active sensing, emissions, deception, decoys, communications jamming, low observability and action-dependent signatures interact without becoming one subsystem. Detection, discrimination, tracking and identification can fail separately.

**Candidate use:** higher TL can mature robustness, fusion, low-probability emissions, cooperative sensing and countermeasure rejection rather than simply escalating one ECM/ECCM number.

## RM-THEME-014 - Exploration quality comes from endurance and consequential discovery

**Sources:** SD-Q06, SD-Q23, SD-Q24

Long-range exploration is interesting when distance, damage, fuel/resources, repairability, unknown hazards and return capability create choices. Sensors/probes should reduce chores while discoveries change options.

**Candidate use:** preserve the press-on/return-home tension even as technology improves. A new route, gate or map connection can be a reward as meaningful as recovered hardware.

## RM-THEME-015 - Biomechanical technology fits existing component/condition architecture

**Sources:** SD-Q13, SD-Q15

Biological/mechanical construction, appearance and sentience are independent. Biological components can be local or pervasive; regeneration is optional rather than inherent.

**Candidate use:** prefer compatibility/support tags, standard component conditions and bounded Damage Control/self-healing effects over a separate bioship rules engine. Sentient ships may create diplomacy/capture/salvage consequences.

## RM-THEME-016 - FTL topology can populate the sector map without replacing ordinary FTL

**Sources:** SD-Q23, SD-Q24

Wormholes, permanent gates, hyperlanes, conduits, one-way links and unstable shortcuts can coexist with a reliable onboard FTL baseline. Their value comes from changing map topology, not from replacing the accepted strategic movement rules.

**Project-discussion synthesis:** this is especially useful for procedural Star Cluster sectors because even a low-TL ship can discover and exploit an extraordinary connection without its own FTL drive gaining an extraordinary TL.

## RM-THEME-017 - Rare infrastructure and Precursor phenomena can break one rule explicitly

**Sources:** SD-SW, SD-Q23, SD-Q24

Exceptional infrastructure or artifacts can be valuable precisely because they violate a normal limitation in a legible, explicit way: unusual range, one-way transit, restricted destination, rare interdiction, or a singular weapon behavior.

**Candidate use:** make the exception itself discoverable content rather than quietly weakening the universal rule whenever convenient.

## RM-THEME-018 - Current future discussion clusters

1. **Damage Control/Hull:** maintainability, bounded Hazards, robotics/self-repair, biomechanical regeneration.
2. **Energy/Beam:** pulsed delivery, focusing/tuning, distributed/phased directors, particle-beam subtypes, specialist countermeasures.
3. **Kinetic:** accelerator/material progression, ammunition sophistication, Macron branch, penetration versus deposition tradeoffs.
4. **Missile/PDS:** separable guidance/warhead progression, swarm/heavy Flight composition, AMMs, local PDS maturation, effective Ammo efficiency.
5. **Sensors/EW/Stealth:** multimodal sensing, cooperative tracks, deception/decoys, comms jamming, anti-emitter counterplay, action-dependent observability.
6. **Power/Thermal:** generation versus storage, fusion/antimatter maturation, thermal-support enabling tags and bounded suppression modes.
7. **Exploration/alien design:** probes, expedition self-sufficiency, biomechanical/sentient ship consequences.
8. **Sector topology/Precursor content:** wormholes, gates, lanes, conduits, rare FTL exceptions and singular superweapon/artifact events.

All clusters remain **discussion candidates**. Promotion requires an explicit human design decision and an update to the authority that owns the result.
