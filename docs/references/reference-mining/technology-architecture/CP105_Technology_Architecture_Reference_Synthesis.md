# CP105 Technology Architecture Reference Synthesis

**Authority:** reference-mining synthesis only. This document records what the source review contributes to CP105; the Star Cluster Concept and explicit human decisions remain authoritative. No external technology tree, proprietary name, statistic or mechanic is imported wholesale.

## 1. Synthesis result

The combined reference corpus strongly supports a **story-first, family-specific technology architecture**. The most reusable pattern is not a nine-step ladder of bigger numbers; it is overlapping generations, enabling-science webs, mature legacy niches, architecture-changing breakthroughs, specialist counters and one-off technologies. This directly supports the CP105 decision to build TL1-TL9 family stories before assigning higher-TL component numbers.

## 2. Preserved Star Cluster and local-reference signals

- **Current Concept v0.7e:** a TL may introduce, mature, miniaturize, specialize or integrate; no family is forced into a repeating three-TL template; older families may improve later through owning or external enabling science. The Power example already uses overlapping Fission/Fusion/Antimatter generations.
- **Spacedock corpus:** repeated support for qualitative progression, separate generation/storage/thermal roles, modular missile development, distinct weapon-family engineering, multimodal/cooperative sensing, specialist countermeasures, automation/robotic repair, and FTL topology as campaign content.
- **Checkpoint 23 reference reconciliation:** broad visible categories with multiple applications, separate STL/FTL components, miniaturization/integration, KISS abstraction and causal compatibility are already long-standing project directions.
- **MOO2 manual:** useful only at the pattern level: one research field can produce multiple applications; later research can create qualitatively different equipment/systems and miniaturization. CP105 does not reuse its categories, names or application sequence.
- **GURPS Space:** its “taxonomy of miracles” is useful as a tone tool. Star Cluster can keep most TL1 engineering slightly futuristic while deliberately granting a few setting miracles (FTL, defensive fields), then increase unfamiliarity at later TLs. Its lists of speculative drives, gravity, fields and exotic matter are question prompts, not adopted technology.

## 3. Terra Invicta official-wiki signals

The user-provided Terra Invicta wiki is most useful as a **lower-SF enabling-web reference**, not as a ladder to copy. Its official/community-maintained pages show multiple fission and fusion architectures coexisting, advanced fission receiving later development, materials such as carbon nanotubes/superalloys/superconductors feeding many subsystem applications, storage technologies distinct from reactors, and photonic/quantum computing as architectural shifts. Those patterns support CP105’s legacy-revival and cross-pollination model.

- Multiple reactor classifications and later **Advanced Fission Systems** demonstrate the *idea* that an old physical principle can receive late engineering development after newer families appear. Star Cluster therefore preserves an Advanced/Optimum Fission revival candidate without copying Terra Invicta’s reactor list.
- Carbon-nanostructure, superalloy and superconducting research in the wiki feeds armor, containment, propulsion, weaponry and storage. CP105 uses that as inspiration for sparse cross-category enabling relationships rather than a universal Materials bonus.
- Supercapacitor and heat-management pages reinforce that storage/discharge and thermal integration are separate engineering axes from sustained reactor output.
- Photonic and quantum computing pages support qualitative computing-principle changes at later eras rather than nine arbitrary targeting-percentage increments.

## 4. Primary-science / engineering grounding

- **NASA Space Nuclear Propulsion:** nuclear thermal and nuclear electric propulsion have complementary thrust/efficiency roles. This supports specialist propulsion branches and mission-dependent technology niches rather than a single “Drive 1 -> Drive 9” replacement chain.
- **NASA Solar Electric Propulsion:** high-efficiency electric propulsion is real but low-thrust, making it good inspiration for probes/logistics/expedition branches rather than the tactical cruiser’s only drive.
- **LLNL NIF:** controlled fusion ignition is a major integration achievement. For Star Cluster, this supports treating Early Fusion, Mature/Compact Fusion and High-Output Fusion as engineering stages rather than merely one reactor with larger output each TL.
- **CERN antimatter:** antimatter is physically real but current production/storage is minute and requires extreme vacuum/electromagnetic trapping. Star Cluster’s higher-SF Antimatter family should therefore retain containment, production/logistics and failure identity even when its gameplay abstraction is simple.

## 5. Theory used only for Exotic/late weird-science capture

- Alcubierre-type warp metrics are retained as science-fantasy/Precursor inspiration, not proof of practical propulsion; the original proposal requires exotic matter.
- Artificial black-hole starship/power-source work is preserved as a deliberately extreme **Exotic** concept. It belongs in the idea register so it is not lost, but not in the normal player ladder.

## 6. Resulting CP105 opportunity clusters

1. **Power:** overlapping Fission -> Fusion -> Antimatter -> matter-conversion families; storage/thermal as side architectures; late Fission revival.
2. **Propulsion:** tactical main-drive lineage plus specialist electric/nuclear branches; FTL remains a separate installed family; fixed routes/gates are infrastructure content.
3. **Materials/Hull/Armor:** materials are a high-value enabler for structure, armor, containment, thermal and accelerators without becoming a new visible research tree.
4. **Weapons:** coherent beams, particle/ion beams, plasma, accelerator branches, smart ammunition and rare pumped/exotic weapons should be distinguished by behavior, not collapsed into one +damage ladder.
5. **Missiles/PDS:** delivery, guidance/seeker, warhead and defensive interception can mature independently.
6. **Information:** multimodal sensors, cooperative tracks, deception, anti-emitter counterplay and signature-management branches create richer progression than one ECM/ECCM escalation.
7. **Repair/automation:** robotics, fabrication and self-healing materials fit the existing condition/Repair Kit model without free regeneration.
8. **Weird science / Precursor:** preserve one-off and rule-breaking concepts in a visible register; do not normalize them simply because the chart has high TLs.

## 8. Originality boundary

CP105 adopts **design questions and scientific relationships**, not external proprietary content. Names such as “Ion Cannon” are treated as generic working labels only; final Star Cluster component names should be original and communicate the gameplay/operating principle. External game statistics, exact prerequisites, technology sequences and formulas are not imported.
