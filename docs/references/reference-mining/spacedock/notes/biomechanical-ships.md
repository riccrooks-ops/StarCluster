- **Source ID:** SD-Q13

- **Status:** mined

## High-value design signals

Biomechanical design is most useful as an alien-technology and narrative spectrum: living status, appearance, construction method and sentience are independent. Biological components and bounded regeneration can fit existing component/condition/compatibility architecture without a separate bioship rules engine.
