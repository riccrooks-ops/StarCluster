# Spacedock Mining Note - Damage Control

- **Source ID:** SD-DC
- **Source:** Spacedock - Damage Control
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkrbbsLNgpU
- **Status:** mined
- **Authority:** reference only; not a Star Cluster rule

## High-value design signals

The source strongly supports the distinction between immediate stabilization/jury-rigging and later proper repair. The most promising new Star Cluster avenues are **repairability-by-design**, a bounded abstraction for ongoing internal **Hazards**, and qualitative Damage Control progression from human teams through automation/robotics to self-repairing materials.

## Observations

### SD-DC-001 - Stabilize first, restore later
- **Timestamp:** 1:03-1:30
- **Source idea:** Immediate damage response is quick and improvised; longer-term patching follows when time permits.
- **Relationship:** corroborates_existing
- **Disposition:** retain_reference
- **Tags:** DAMAGE_CONTROL, QUALITATIVE_TECH_PROGRESSION
- **Assessment:** Strong fictional grounding for Star Cluster's combat Damage Control versus later Ship Repair distinction. Do not add another repair layer merely to mirror the source.

### SD-DC-002 - Distributed damage-control stores
- **Timestamp:** 1:17-1:38
- **Source idea:** Repair tools and consumables are most useful when distributed and immediately accessible in dedicated lockers.
- **Relationship:** extends_candidate
- **Disposition:** discuss
- **Tags:** DAMAGE_CONTROL, SHIP_ARCHITECTURE, LOGISTICS
- **Assessment:** Repair Kits can plausibly abstract these stores. A future maintainability or repair-capacity trait could reflect better distribution without inventorying individual lockers.

### SD-DC-003 - Computer casualties can be physical or logical
- **Timestamp:** 2:51-3:44
- **Source idea:** A controlled system may fail because computer hardware is damaged or because software/control logic fails even when the machinery itself remains physically intact.
- **Relationship:** new_candidate
- **Disposition:** defer
- **Tags:** COMPUTING, DAMAGE_CONTROL, CYBERWARFARE
- **Assessment:** Useful future Computer/Damage Control/cyber crossover. Ordinary combat damage should remain component-condition abstraction rather than adding board/software fault types.

### SD-DC-004 - Ongoing internal hazards
- **Timestamp:** 3:51-6:34
- **Source idea:** Fire, atmosphere loss, leaking propellants and related casualties can continue worsening conditions until isolated or suppressed.
- **Relationship:** new_candidate
- **Disposition:** discuss
- **Tags:** DAMAGE_CONTROL, INTERNAL_DAMAGE, AVOID_EXCESS_SIMULATION
- **Assessment:** Candidate for a bounded Hazard/escalation state competing with ordinary repair capacity. Avoid deck-by-deck fire, oxygen, coolant, or smoke simulation.

### SD-DC-005 - Emergency isolation can trade local loss for ship survival

- **Relationship:** new_candidate

- **Assessment:** Potential memorable command decision if a future Hazard/crew model supports it. Do not invent the casualty model prematurely.

## Candidate discussion queue

1. Repairability/maintainability as a ship-design property.
2. A bounded internal Hazard system that competes with repair effort without becoming a compartment simulator.
3. Qualitative Damage Control technology progression.
4. Emergency isolation only after crew/Hazard abstractions are mature.
