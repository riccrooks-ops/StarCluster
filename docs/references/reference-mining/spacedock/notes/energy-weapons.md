# Spacedock Mining Note - Energy Weapons

- **Source ID:** SD-EW
- **Source:** Spacedock - Energy Weapons
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N1RnANxhPAM
- **Status:** mined
- **Authority:** reference only; not a Star Cluster rule

## High-value design signals

This source focuses on laser wavelength/frequency. Its strongest contribution is a **beam/focusing progression axis** and the possibility of higher-TL **tunable/adaptive-spectrum** Energy weapons. It also strongly corroborates Star Cluster's separation of physical weapon reach from sensor/track attack eligibility.

## Observations

### SD-EW-001 - Physical laser reach is not attack eligibility
- **Timestamp:** 1:03-1:27
- **Source idea:** Light-speed propagation gives enormous physical reach, but diffraction, jitter, sensor resolution and target evasion limit useful engagement.
- **Relationship:** corroborates_existing
- **Disposition:** retain_reference
- **Tags:** ENERGY_WEAPONS, ENGAGEMENT_ENVELOPE, SENSORS
- **Assessment:** Strong external support for keeping physical weapon range, sensor reach, track quality and attack eligibility distinct.
