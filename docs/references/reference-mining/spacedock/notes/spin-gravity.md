## High-value design signals

Because Star Cluster has not yet established an artificial-gravity architecture, this source raises a **foundational setting/design question** rather than supplying an immediate mechanic. The strongest candidates are gravity as Hull/habitation architecture, cruise-spin versus combat-secured configuration, and generated gravity as a later architectural breakthrough that removes design constraints rather than merely granting a crew bonus.

## Candidate discussion queue

1. Decide the setting's gravity architecture deliberately rather than by assumption.
2. Treat baseline gravity/habitation primarily as Hull/ship architecture, not an obligatory Auxiliary tax.
3. Explore cruise-spin/combat-secured warship fiction.
4. Consider generated gravity as a design-envelope breakthrough while preserving useful spin-gravity alternatives.
